Our Journey

Milestones that shaped us

From a two-person garage workshop to one of America's most trusted mobility brands.

2001

The Beginning

Daniel Reyes founds Home Elevator after his grandfather's fall. Working out of a garage, he installs the first straight stairlift in Portland, Oregon.

2009

First Home Elevator

We install our first residential elevator — a compact shaftless system — and launch our commercial division serving boutique hotels and offices.

2016

National Expansion

We open regional hubs in Los Angeles and Chicago, expanding service to 15 states. Our install count passes 500 projects.

2022

Smart Mobility Era

We launch app-connected elevators, regenerative drives, and remote diagnostics. Our systems become 40% more energy-efficient.

2026

Today

Over 1,200 installations completed. A team of 80+ engineers, designers, and technicians serving families and businesses across America.
