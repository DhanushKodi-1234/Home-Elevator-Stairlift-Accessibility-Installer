How long does a typical stairlift installation take?

Most straight stairlift installations are completed in 2–4 hours. Curved stairlifts require a custom rail fabricated to your staircase, so installation typically takes a full day. We handle all mounting, wiring, testing, and cleanup.

Do I need a permit for a home elevator?

Yes — most jurisdictions require a permit for residential elevators. The good news: our in-house compliance team handles all paperwork, load calculations, and coordination with your local building department. You don't lift a finger.

What warranty do you offer?

All installations come with a 5-year comprehensive warranty covering parts and labor, plus a lifetime warranty on structural rails. Optional extended service plans are available with annual maintenance visits.

Can you install in historic or older buildings?

Absolutely. We specialize in retrofits for brownstones, Victorian homes, and landmark buildings. Custom rail shapes, low-profile designs, and finishes that match existing woodwork are all part of our offering.

Do you offer financing?

Yes — we partner with leading lenders to offer 0% financing for up to 12 months and extended plans up to 60 months. Your consultant can walk you through options during the free site visit.
