Customization FAQs

Everything you need to know about the bespoke process, lead times, and options.

Book a design call

How long does a bespoke build take?

Typical custom cabins are crafted in 4–8 weeks after design approval. Stairlift customization is faster — usually 2–4 weeks. Installation itself adds 1–5 days depending on the scope.

Can I provide my own reference images?

Absolutely — we love it when clients share Pinterest boards, interior photos, or fabric samples. Our design team will translate your aesthetic into a cabin concept that fits your home's existing character.

What if I don't like the 3D render?

You'll receive unlimited revisions until the render matches your vision. Nothing is built until you sign off — this is the whole point of the render phase.

Does customization affect the warranty?

No — any customization we perform is covered under the same 5–10 year warranty as our standard products. We only use certified components and finishes that meet our quality standards.

Is customization expensive?

It depends entirely on scope. A color change might add a few hundred dollars; a full custom cabin with premium materials can add several thousand. We'll always provide a transparent line-item quote before any commitment.
