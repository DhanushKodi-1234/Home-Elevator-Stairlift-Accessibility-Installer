Common Questions

Frequently Asked

How long does installation take?

Most straight stairlifts install in 2–4 hours. Curved stairlifts take 1–2 days. Home elevators take 3–7 days depending on design scope.

Do I need to modify my home structurally?

Not usually. Most of our lifts are designed for retrofit installation with minimal structural change. Our engineers confirm this during the free site survey.

What if the power goes out?

Every lift ships with battery backup. In a power outage, your lift safely completes its current trip and remains available for at least 24 hours.

Is financing available?

Yes — we offer 0% APR for 12 months and extended 60-month financing. Your consultant will walk you through all options during the quote.

What does the warranty cover?

5–10 year comprehensive warranty on parts and labor, plus lifetime warranty on structural rails. Annual service plans are optional.
